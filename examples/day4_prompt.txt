Day 4 (Thursday): Adventure Story
* Focus Skill: Using time connectives (first, then, next, after that, finally) to order events clearly, plus exclamation marks for excitement in dialogue.
* Topic: The Day My School Bag Grew Wings and Flew Over the Whole City of Dhaka
* Story Starter: "When the bell rang at the end of the day, I picked up my school bag and it suddenly began to flap its enormous, feathery wings..."
* Chat Prompts:
  1. Where would it fly first?
  2. Who saw it?
  3. How did it end?
* Word Vault: `soared` = flew high, `whooshed` (moved fast with a sound), `gasped`, `fluttered` (flapped softly), `astonished` (very, very surprised)
* Challenge: Use "first", "then" and "finally".
